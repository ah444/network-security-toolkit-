Other computers

This toolkit is open to your network. Anyone with the access token has your rights over the saved runs, bundles and scans, not only a view of the pages: they can upload data, train models and run port scans from this computer.

A new token locks every other computer out at once, without stopping the toolkit: the jobs they asked for that still wait are cancelled, a running one goes on and can be stopped below. The new token is shown in the terminal where the toolkit was started.
