# v5.2 Implementation Checklist — "The user's improvements"

Day-to-day driver for v5.2. The **why** behind each item is in [IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md](IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md) (§7 roadmap "v5.2"; IDs in brackets). v5.1 is done: [V5.1_CHECKLIST.md](V5.1_CHECKLIST.md), tag `5.1`.
Rules:
- Same as v5.1: a test first, then the smallest change, then commit. Nothing starts without the user's approval of its step, and each step is proposed first.
- "Green" = `python -m pytest`, ruff and mypy pass on the **Windows** PC with **Python 3.14**. Linux/macOS: portability rules as in v5.0, not tested on real machines.
- The pages work on whatever browser the user has: nothing they need may depend on a recent browser feature or a download (plan D2; the user's choice in 2.1).
- Real-data checks: the three tiers of v5.0 (fixtures for every change, samples per step, full files only at the tag gate).
- `pip-audit` (environment and `requirements.lock`) runs at the end of every step and at every tag.
