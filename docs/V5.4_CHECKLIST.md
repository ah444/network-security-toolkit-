## F6 History [F6]
Approved 2026-11-03, as proposed. The trainings already have their history: every successful training saves its record (HR5), and Saved Runs lists, opens, compares and deletes them, on the page and in the CLI (F2, HR4). F6 gives the scans the same. Decisions:
- **Every finished scan is saved on its own**, the CLI's and the web's, like every training (RD3); F10 and the comparison need them. It replaces 5.2's 4a (the last web scan in the session only)
- **Where:** `instance/scan_history/<scan id>.json`, apart from the reports the user saves in `instance/scans/`; the id is the one of the scan's lines in the log (HS3), the time it started and 8 hex digits, like a training run's; the file published whole (HR3a), written once, never changed; refused on a full disk (HR4c), and the scan then says it was not saved
- **What:** the request (the target as typed, its addresses, the ports, the protocols, the engine and nmap's choices, where it came from), what could go online and why not, how each host ended (scanned; skipped: it didn't answer the ping; failed, with why), AbuseIPDB's answers, the report entries (how each CVE was matched, the AI's text marked "may be wrong", the lookup errors, the OS hint) and the totals. Never a key or a token
- **A cancelled scan, or one stopped by the time limit, saves nothing**, as a cancelled training keeps nothing
- **Nothing is deleted on its own:** the user deletes; Storage counts them and warns as the space grows (HR4b, HR4c)
- **The comparison of two scans of the same target**, port by port: opened, closed, the service or the version changed, CVEs added or gone, the highest CVSS changed. A port that the other scan didn't check is "not scanned there", not closed; CVEs not looked up in one scan (NVD off) are "not checked", not gone; different targets: the addresses both have only, and the page says so
